隱私權政策

單字樂園・最後更新：2026 年 11 月 16 日・English

我們收集哪些資料

不收集。本 App 沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具，也不會把你的答題、分數或任何紀錄傳到任何地方。

存在你裝置上的資料

遊戲進度、學習紀錄（單字護照、救援站、每日印章、連續天數、成就徽章、錯字本、道具）、你輸入的自訂單字，以及語言、音效與提醒等設定，只儲存在這台裝置的本機儲存空間。刪除 App 即會一併刪除。

備份碼

在「設定」裡可以產生一段備份碼，用來把學習紀錄搬到另一台裝置。備份碼只在你的手機上產生，由你自己複製和保存；我們不會收到它，也不會把它傳到任何伺服器。

通知

你可以自行開啟「每日複習提醒」和單字營火的「入侵者提醒」。這些都是在手機上排程的本機通知，不經過任何伺服器，需要你同意通知權限才會開啟，不同意也能正常使用。

連線到的第三方服務

本 App 不會連線到任何伺服器。圖形、字體與音效都內建在 App 裡；單字發音使用 iPhone 內建的語音合成，在手機上產生。

兒童隱私

本 App 不收集任何人的個人資料，包括兒童。

Privacy Policy

Word Park · Last updated: November 16, 2026

What data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, and it never sends your answers, scores or any other record anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, learning records (Word Passport, Rescue Station, daily stamps, day streak, badges, mistake book, items), custom words you enter, and settings such as language, sound and reminders are stored only in local storage on this device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Backup code

Settings can create a backup code so you can move your learning record to another device. The code is generated on your phone and copied and kept by you. We never receive it and it is not sent to any server.

Notifications

You can choose to turn on a daily review reminder and, in Word Campfire, an invader alert. These are local notifications scheduled on your phone. They do not go through any server, they need your permission, and the app works normally without them.

Third-party services

The app does not connect to any server. Graphics, fonts and sounds are built in, and words are spoken with the speech synthesis built into your iPhone.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
